4. EVALUATION SCORECARD (SCORE INDEPENDENTLY BEFORE DISCUSSION)
Agree criterion weights before demos; use the same weights for each vendor.
Weights must total 100. Put mandatory requirements in gates, not just weights.

Evidence scale:
0 = Not shown
1 = Stated only
2 = Partly demonstrated
3 = Demonstrated with assumptions
4 = Demonstrated end to end with required evidence

For each criterion record weight / score / evidence / assumptions / confidence:
Process fit:
User effort:
Controls and reporting:
Integration recovery:
Delivery and partner fit:
Additional criterion, if required:

Weighted result = sum of (criterion weight x score / 4).
Example: weight 20 and score 3 contribute 15 points.
A failed or unproven mandatory gate prevents approval regardless of the total.
Compare the evidence and unresolved assumptions alongside the score.

5. AUSTRALIAN WORKFLOW PROMPTS (ADAPT TO YOUR SCOPE)
- GST-coded transactions, BAS reporting task, reconciliation, close correction.
- Payroll and Peppol provider hand-offs, failed transfer, support ownership.
- Partial receipt, backorder, return, warehouse and ecommerce reconciliation.
- Intercompany or project-cost exception, approvals, reporting dimensions.
- Australian support coverage, hosting, privacy, and data-export assumptions.
These are acceptance-test prompts, not a guarantee of legal compliance.
